> [!abstract]+ The Invest tab: market mode, chart page, rows, live data
> Part of [[Design Rules]] (read the core first). Charts: [[Rules - Charts]]. The investing rules it checks (cap, batches, emergency fund) are set in Investment Config. Every Invest page carries `.zl-invest` (`view.ts`), so up / down colours apply to all of them and to the full chart.

## 🏠 Invest home = market mode (owner's artifact rounds 1–10, Vellum screen "Invest home · market mode"; built 2026-11-10)
- The board (what you hold is worth; today's move on the right in up / down; Gain / loss as a split-flap with "+10.2% since bought" under its label, net after the Taiwan sell fee and tax) → the **market card** → the **watchlist marquee** (always there: the market card's chart, at least 220 px, gives up the room; owner, 2026-11-13) → the links on one line, titles only, each centred in its button: **HOLDINGS | MAP | ALERTS | MIX** (no counts, no warnings text: Mix shows them; owner, 2026-11-13, replacing the two-across links of 2026-11-11). Sideways: the marquee's cells (no head) under the board on the left, the links under the market card on the right.
- **Market card** (`ui/market.ts`; owner 2026-11-10: only an index's chart, with the ticker chart's interaction): tabs TAIEX · SOX · NASDAQ (a dot on the ones whose market trades), the index and today's % as a small split-flap, then the ticker chart (no interval strip, a pinch walks TODAY → 5 → 15 → 60 → D; MA20 · BB · MA60 small toggles; a sideways drag pans; hold for the crosshair; no tools, no full chart, no volume pane). The lead **rolls by itself**: TAIEX while Taiwan trades, SOX while New York trades, else the market that closed last; a tab tapped holds until the lead would roll next; the card goes back to TODAY when its market opens.
- **Watchlist marquee:** cells as wide as their content (12 px each side); in a cell the ticker top left, the price top right, today's change bottom right; motion and touch: [[Rules - Controls]] › A strip that runs by itself; a tap opens the chart.
- **MIX** is its own page: cash waiting and still to buy this month, the mix bars with the warnings head, this month's plan per bucket. **MAP**: the market map ([[Rules - Charts]]).
- A sideways swipe anywhere on Invest home stays in the page; the page never redraws under a finger on the chart or the marquee.

## 📈 Chart page (a holding or any ticker)
- Last price (label, today's change line, then for a Taiwan stock the **sector · TAIEX** line and under it the **REL** line: the stock's % − the sector's %; both keep their room while empty; 2026-11-10 REL got its own line, it was cut beside a full price) with the price split-flap on the right → source line with CANDLE · FOOTPRINT · HEAT (Taiwan) → the chart, which takes all the room left (`height: 0`, `zl-c-fill zl-chg`) → a holding's one-line strip (You hold · Average cost · Value now with %) → tiles: 三大法人 (its day and 張 in up / down) · Trades · Plan · Journal; a ticker: ON THE WATCHLIST (eye) · 三大法人. Trades is a C list page; Plan opens its sheet. CHART in the masthead opens the chart settings sheet.
- The price is "Current price" while its market trades, "Last price" before the open (one rule: `prices.isOpen` with the market calendar).

## 🧭 Long-term tools (owner picked 2026-11-10; Vellum screens "Invest · chart page with thesis", "rules check before a buy", "monthly revenue", "alerts"; built 2026-11-10)
- **THESIS card** on every chart page, between the chart and the tiles (the chart keeps taking the room left): WHY, NEXT CHECK (what must hold, its date, in N days; loud from 7 days before), SELL IF, vs 0050 since watching (up / down; its line kept while the day bars load). On or after the check day it asks **DID IT HOLD? YES · NO**, writes the answer into the journal and opens the sheet for the next check. None written: one loud line **WRITE THE THESIS ›**. Kept in `Invest/<ticker>.md` as `thesis: { why, check, hold, sell, since }` (`since` = the first save). Sideways (the full chart) it is one line beside the MA chips; a tap floats the card over the chart.
- **Rules check before a buy**: TRADE on a chart page opens it first: thesis, sell rule, room under the cap, batch (n of 3, about cap / 3), emergency fund ≥ `emergency_min` or the `emergency` account's target, limit price (sets the trade sheet's price), satellites vs 0050. Met is ink, not met is loud with what to do; it informs, never blocks: CONTINUE always goes on. A holding gets SELL beside CONTINUE (a sale skips the rules). Rules: `calc/thesis.ts`.
- **REVENUE tile** (Taiwan stocks, not ETFs): last month against a year before in up / down, NEW until its page is opened. The page: the month as the number with vs a year before and vs the month before, the last 24 months as bars you pick from (a strip of each month's YoY under them), then last 3 months vs a year ago, this year so far, best month, next out (opens Alerts). Data: FinMind monthly revenue, kept on the device, asked again at most every 3 hours while the month that should be out is missing.
- **ALERTS** (link on Invest home beside MAP; a C list page): ALL · PRICES · DATES; price alerts (at or below / at or above, how far away), revenue out, report deadlines (Mar 31, May 15, Aug 14, Nov 14), ex-dividend (TWSE TWT48U) and thesis checks for holdings and the watchlist; SENT for 7 days. Checked once a minute while Ledgerline runs, dates from 08:00; each sent once as a notice and, with `topic:` in `Config/Alerts.md`, a push through ntfy.

## 🧾 Rows and the change line
- **The change line** (owner, 2026-11-04, like Apple's Stocks): over a ticker's value sits its change, small, in up / down; a tap on it cycles every change line on the page together: today's % → today's price change (the stock's currency) → your profit (holdings only; the watchlist stays on the price change); remembered on the device.
- **Holdings and Watchlist rows** (the owner's row, Vellum screen "Invest · Watchlist row"): the ticker big on the left with shares / name beside it on the same level, the change as a small split-flap top right (tiles 14 × 24; the + / − on its own tile, raised 2 px; digits in the tile up / down colours; it flips from the old number when the price moves) with the value under it. Holdings has search; BUY amounts are not loud.
- Money: one minus sign (U+2212) and one price format everywhere; a new ticker's currency comes from its market.

## 📡 Live data
- **Badge** beside every Invest title: **TW TRADING** / **US TRADING** names the market that trades and blinks its dot once per update; while none trades it names the next open of your tickers' markets, **US OPENS 22:30** (today) or **TW OPENS MON 09:00** (a later day), in device time; a weekday in the usual hours with the market shut reads **TW CLOSED · TYPHOON** / **US CLOSED · HOLIDAY** (also NO TRADING, EARLY CLOSE): `calc/calendar.ts`. **Opening bell:** the first update after the open sweeps the board once.
- **Live tick** (every 5 s at most while a market trades): Invest home, Holdings and Watchlist redraw under the no-blink rules; chart and holding pages change in place; other Invest pages show no live price. Shared code: `liveTick()` in `ui/c.ts`, `FlipDot.set`, `view.ts` `pricesMoved`.
- **Sources:** Taiwan stocks and TAIEX from TWSE MIS / Fugle (live), the TAIEX TODAY chart from the 5-second file; SOX, NASDAQ and US stocks from Yahoo. Any value that is late carries a delay tag; LIVE only when all is live (Source fix, open).
