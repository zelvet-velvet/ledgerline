> [!abstract]+ Every chart: the ticker chart, the index card, tape plots, flows bars, the map
> Part of [[Design Rules]] (read the core first). Loading, switching and live motion: [[Rules - Motion]] › Charts. A new chart reuses `ui/chart.ts` (candles, lines) or the block charts in `ui/c.ts`; it never draws its own axes, crosshair or loading.

## 🕯️ The ticker chart (`ui/chart.ts`; any ticker, a holding, the index card, the full chart)
- **Candles and lines** (owner's round with reference screenshots, 2026-11-04): up / down in `--zl-up` / `--zl-down` (core › Up / down colours; red up by default), volume bars the same. MB 20 (BB's middle, the 20-day average; no separate MA20) amber, MA60 blue, BB a faint band with muted edges, average cost a dashed ink line (day / week / month only scale to it). A source without OHLC draws a close line.
- **Legend strip** (owner, 2026-11-05; replaced the MA / BB chips above the plot): under the tools, three equal cells MB 20 · BB 2σ · MA60 split by hairlines; each cell is the toggle for its line (a swatch drawn like the line, pixel name over the value at the held bar, else the last); on = name and value in its colour, off = dimmed, hollow swatch. BB 2σ shows LB–UB and σ. Under it **one line that never moves**: the date first, then O H L C and the move (a close line: the close only), tool hints in its place. The index card uses small toggles MA20 · BB · MA60 right of that line instead (`is-chips`).
- **Intervals** (chart page): one segmented strip M W D 60 30 15 5 1 TODAY (owner, 2026-11-06: longest to shortest, TODAY at the right end), the selected one inverted. One screen = a fixed number of bars (1: 120, 5: 96, 15: 80, 30: 64, 60: 60, D: 66, W: 78, M: 60). **TODAY** = the whole session's width (Taiwan 270 minutes, else 390) in 5-minute candles on every chart, with the previous close dashed ("prev 100.60") and the K effects on the forming candle while the market trades (owner, 2026-11-10). Minute charts refresh every minute while on screen. The index card has no strip: a pinch walks TODAY → 5 → 15 → 60 → D, each step the same span of time.
- **Price axis on the left, labels right of it** (owner, 2026-11-06): the axis line at the left edge, its labels just inside the plot (a halo over the bars), so the bars take the whole width: 4–5 nice ticks (1, 2, 2.5, 5 × 10ⁿ), Plex Mono 11, the currency on top. The last price is a loud tag just right of the latest bar with a dotted line back to the axis, and a gap that wide is always kept after the latest bar (owner, 2026-11-10). The window's highest high and lowest low printed beside their candles, pointing inward.
- **Time axis:** minute charts: hours ("10:00"), a new day by its date ("Nov 4"); D: months ("SEP"); W: quarters (January = the year); M: years. Labels stay clear of both edges and of each other. US bars at New York's clock (DST included).
- **Volume pane** under the price pane (chart page and full chart; not on the index card): bars in up / down, VOL5 ink and VOL10 loud lines, "VOL 1.9M VOL5 … VOL10 …" (short numbers); no pane when no volume is known.
- **Fixed size:** the chart never changes height (legend, the one line, plot); loading as in Motion, so nothing below moves.
- **Touch:** hold 0.35 s = crosshair (dashed lines, a price tag on the axis, a time tag on the time axis; the page does not scroll under it); a sideways drag pans (LATEST › to come back); a **pinch** zooms (12 bars up to all of them), the candle between the fingers stays put, each interval keeps its zoom; TODAY zooms too. A still tap draws when a tool is on. **Gestures belong to the chart**: a drag on the plot never scrolls the page, and Obsidian's edge swipes / pull-down are off on chart pages and in the full chart. New bars redraw the chart only, never the page.
- **K effects** (`ui/keffects.ts`, thresholds in `calc/keffects.ts`, tested; grow with √interval): only on TODAY's forming candle while the market trades: break dot with chips, BB edge fades in within 1.5 σ then cracks with shards along the band's slope, long-candle sparks (2 × the last 20 bodies and ≥ 0.25 %) in `--zl-spark` (steel, never up / down), a small mark on closed candles that broke. A held crosshair keeps them; a pinch or a finger on the plot pauses them; no shake (owner); no limit lock on indices.
- **Tools** FIB / LINE / LEVEL (and RANGE in the full chart), the armed one inverted, it stays armed. **One drawing at a time**: two taps draw (LEVEL one), the next tap on a drawn chart clears it; no CLEAR button. Fib levels from B back toward A (price = B − (B − A) × r), labels "61.8%  51.20" right-aligned inside the plot; 50 and 61.8 loud with a light band between them, others muted; the A→B leg dashed loud with square handles. Drawings live in data units (date or minute, price in hundredths), only while the page is open. The CHART sheet (Fib levels, snap, cost, MA) is saved in `Invest/<ticker>.md`.
- **Live** while a chart is on screen: a quote every 5 s (once a minute when the quote is over 15 minutes old); the split-flap, today's change, the price tag and the last candle update in place (a new minute starts a new candle).
- **Full chart:** the phone turned, or "FULL CHART ›" (from an upright phone the layer is drawn rotated 90°): left rail 64 px (‹ FIB LINE LEVEL RANGE), top bar with the ticker, the read-out line and the intervals. RANGE measures between two taps ("+N (+x%) · N bars"), not saved.

## 🔥 Tape plots: footprint, heat, net orders (`pages/invest-flow.ts`)
- Taiwan stock charts switch **CANDLE · FOOTPRINT · HEAT** with a boxed segment right of the source line (chosen inverted); every Taiwan stock has it; under 1,000 lots a day (5-day average) the read-out says THIN.
- The tape comes from Fugle (whole day + stream) or TWSE MIS 5 s steps; bid × ask cells, POC, value area (edges labelled VA), imbalances, the 5-level book (ASK / BID), a BOOK pressure strip under the plot.
- A range bar over the plot: 1 / 5 / 15 min bars and 30M / 1H / DAY (small boxed segment), LATEST › on the right kept hidden until the plot is panned.
- Touch: hold = crosshair with the cell's numbers in the read-out (bold), drag sideways = back in time, pinch = the next range step; the read-out lines keep their height.
- Hints: a loud chip top right of the plot when something is worth a look; a tap shows its explanation floating over the plot's foot (Motion › Floating hint).

## 📦 Block charts and bars
- **法人 flows bars** (2026-11-04): one stacked bar per day around a solid zero line, buying up and selling down, 外資 ink, 投信 muted, 自營商 hatched (muted for ETFs: hedging); in lots (張), axis in K / M; a tap or drag picks a day for the line above. Flows pages are one screen (the chart takes the room left); detail per 外資 / 投信 / 自營商 has its own bars (bought / sold in up / down on Invest; 自營商 own trading solid, hedging hatched).
- **Market map:** squarified tiles (size = last day's trading value, or the watchlist), labels fitted, small ones folded into a +N tile; tap = numbers, tap again = open; whole-market net orders per 5 min under it.
- **Block charts** on the other tabs (waffle, staircase, budget bars, towers, balance beam): squares in the C grid, lit as in Motion; Log's budget lines carry no day-of-month tick, the words say what is left.
- **A bar chart someone picks from** (flows days, a month of revenue): a tap or a drag across the bars picks one; the picked bar is ink, the others muted, the line above or the rows under show its numbers; the page never scrolls during the drag.
