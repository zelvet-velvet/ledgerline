> [!abstract]+ The Invest tab: market mode, chart page, rows, live data
> Part of [[Design Rules]] (read the core first). Charts: [[Rules - Charts]]. The investing rules it checks (cap, batches, emergency fund) are set in Investment Config. Every Invest page carries `.zl-invest` (`view.ts`), so up / down colours apply to all of them and to the full chart.
>
> The mock screens these rules name ("2330 · …", "Chart · view and draw row", "Split-flaps …") were removed once built (2026-11-18); they stay in git history. Vellum keeps the captured real pages: Holding chart (2330) and Holding chart · ETF (0050).

## 🏠 Invest home = market mode (owner's artifact rounds 1–10, Vellum screen "Invest home · market mode"; built 2026-11-10)
- The board (what you hold is worth; today's move on the right in up / down; Gain / loss as a split-flap with "+10.2% since bought" under its label, net after the Taiwan sell fee and tax) → the **market card** → the **watchlist marquee** (always there: the market card's chart, at least 220 px, gives up the room; owner, 2026-11-13) → the links on one line, titles only, each centred in its button: **HOLDINGS | MAP | ALERTS | MIX** (no counts, no warnings text: Mix shows them; owner, 2026-11-13, replacing the two-across links of 2026-11-11). Sideways: the marquee's cells (no head) under the board on the left, the links under the market card on the right.
- **Market card** (`ui/market.ts`; owner 2026-11-10: only an index's chart, with the ticker chart's interaction): tabs TAIEX · SOX · NASDAQ (a dot on the ones whose market trades), the index and today's % as a small split-flap, then the ticker chart (no interval strip, a pinch walks TODAY → 5 → 15 → 60 → D; MA20 · BB · MA60 small toggles; a sideways drag pans; hold for the crosshair; no tools, no full chart, no volume pane). The lead **rolls by itself**: TAIEX while Taiwan trades, SOX while New York trades, else the market that closed last; a tab tapped holds until the lead would roll next; the card goes back to TODAY when its market opens.
- **Watchlist marquee:** cells as wide as their content (12 px each side); in a cell the ticker top left, the price top right, today's change bottom right; motion and touch: [[Rules - Controls]] › A strip that runs by itself; a tap opens the chart.
- **MIX** is its own page: cash waiting and still to buy this month, the mix bars with the warnings head, this month's plan per bucket. **MAP**: the market map ([[Rules - Charts]]).
- A sideways swipe anywhere on Invest home stays in the page; the page never redraws under a finger on the chart or the marquee.

## 📈 Chart page (a holding or any ticker)
- The top as Vellum "2330 · top A ruled rows" (owner picked 2026-11-18): the price row (CURRENT / LAST PRICE as a small label, today's change a size up in its colour, the price split-flap on the right), then for a Taiwan stock one row of three cells between thin rules, label left and value right: **sector · TAIEX · REL** (REL: the stock's % − the sector's %), or for one with no sector (an ETF like 0050) **TAIEX · VS TAIEX**; the cells keep their room while empty → a holding's strip, four across as ruled cells like the market row (a thin line between them, the label at each cell's upper left, the value against its right edge) (You hold · Avg cost · Value with % · **Plan**: the next plan date, a tap opens the plan sheet; under the price, owner's edit of Vellum C1, 2026-11-18) → the chart as Vellum "2330 · page D2 controls under the chart" (owner picked 2026-11-18): the readout line with THESIS › at its end, the MA line (MB · BB · MA60 on one line, a tap switches a line), the source and its time small over the plot, the plot taking all the room left (`height: 0`, `zl-c-fill zl-chg`), then by the thumb the ranges (M … TODAY) and one row: CANDLE · FOOTPRINT · HEAT (Taiwan) as a compact switch and one ✎ DRAW ▾ button, which swaps the row for FIB · LINE · LEVEL and DONE while drawing (Vellum "Chart · view and draw row" R4, owner picked 2026-11-18); the row hangs under the chart or the tape, so FOOTPRINT and HEAT always have the way back, and DRAW goes faint off the candles → groups 10 px apart → tiles in one row as a ruled strip (Vellum "2330 · tiles E4a framed", owner picked 2026-11-18: a rule over and under, thin lines between, each cell centred: the label with a small ›, the big value, its small line; no boxes, no icons): 三大法人 (the number in up / down with a small 張 beside it, the report day under it as "Nov 17") · Revenue · Trades · Journal; a ticker: ON THE WATCHLIST (eye) · 三大法人 · Revenue. Trades is a C list page. Owner picked mock B, 2026-11-18 (the page was 130 px over its room: the thesis card and a second tile row). CHART in the masthead opens the chart settings sheet.

## 🧭 Long-term tools (owner picked 2026-11-10; Vellum screens "Invest · chart page with thesis", "rules check before a buy", "monthly revenue", "alerts"; built 2026-11-10)
- **THESIS** on every chart page as a small outlined **THESIS ›** button at the right end of the chart's readout line (Vellum "2330 · C1 thesis on the readout line", owner picked 2026-11-18: no strip, the chart takes its room); a loud dot on it when the check is due or within 7 days; with none written it reads **WRITE ›** in loud and opens the sheet. A tap floats the card over the chart. On the full chart (sideways) the thesis is one line beside the MA chips, and only when one is written. The card: WHY, NEXT CHECK (what must hold, its date, in N days; loud from 7 days before), SELL IF, vs 0050 since watching (up / down; its line kept while the day bars load). On or after the check day it asks **DID IT HOLD? YES · NO**, writes the answer into the journal and opens the sheet for the next check. None written: one loud line **WRITE THE THESIS ›**. Kept in `Invest/<ticker>.md` as `thesis: { why, check, hold, sell, since }` (`since` = the first save). Sideways (the full chart) the same line sits beside the MA chips.
- **Rules check before a buy**: TRADE on a chart page opens it first: thesis, sell rule, room under the cap (`single_stock_cap`: a share of the stock bucket, "40%" = of stock cash + stock cost, or a flat amount; Mix › Rules says which; a tap on its SINGLE-STOCK CAP row opens the cap sheet: Share of stock | Flat amount, one number with the pad, "= 10,100 now: 40% of stock cash … + stock cost …" under it, SAVE writes `single_stock_cap`; owner picked Vellum "Single-stock cap · share", 2026-11-20; screen "Single-stock cap"), batch (n of 3, about cap / 3), emergency fund ≥ `emergency_min` or the `emergency` account's target, limit price (sets the trade sheet's price), satellites vs 0050. Met is ink, not met is loud with what to do; it informs, never blocks: CONTINUE always goes on. A holding gets SELL beside CONTINUE (a sale skips the rules). Rules: `calc/thesis.ts`.
- **A ticker not held yet in the trade sheet** (owner picked Vellum "Trade · category A", 2026-11-20): under BUY OR SELL a CATEGORY row, the boxed segment of the mix's buckets with a guess already on, never "other": Taiwan from the ticker (00…B a bond ETF, 00… an ETF, else a stock), elsewhere an ETF when the search said so, else a stock; "TWSE stock · guessed" on the label's line while the guess is on. The category is what the mix, the cap warning and the cash account read. A held ticker has no such row. Screen: "Trade · a ticker not held". Maths: `guessCategory` in `calc/invest.ts`.
- **REVENUE tile** (Taiwan stocks, not ETFs): last month against a year before in up / down, NEW until its page is opened. The page: the month as the number with vs a year before and vs the month before, the last 24 months as bars you pick from (a strip of each month's YoY under them), then last 3 months vs a year ago, this year so far, best month, next out (opens Alerts). Data: FinMind monthly revenue, kept on the device, asked again at most every 3 hours while the month that should be out is missing.
- **ALERTS** (link on Invest home beside MAP; a C list page): ALL · PRICES · DATES; price alerts (at or below / at or above, how far away), revenue out, report deadlines (Mar 31, May 15, Aug 14, Nov 14), ex-dividend (TWSE TWT48U) and thesis checks for holdings and the watchlist; SENT for 7 days. Checked once a minute while Ledgerline runs, dates from 08:00; each sent once as a notice and, with `topic:` in `Config/Alerts.md`, a push through ntfy.

## 🧾 Rows and the change line
- **The change line** (owner, 2026-11-04, like Apple's Stocks): over a ticker's value sits its change, small, in up / down; a tap on it cycles every change line on the page together: today's % → today's price change (the stock's currency) → your profit (holdings only; the watchlist stays on the price change); remembered on the device.
- **Holdings and Watchlist rows** (the owner's row, Vellum screen "Invest · Watchlist row"): the ticker big on the left with shares / name beside it on the same level, the change as a small split-flap top right (tiles 14 × 24; the + / − on its own tile, its bar just under the seam (Rules - Motion › Split-flaps); digits in the tile up / down colours; it flips from the old number when the price moves) with the value under it. Holdings has search; BUY amounts are not loud. **A Taiwan row at today's limit** (owner picked Vellum "Watchlist · limit rows C1", 2026-11-24, #18; the whole row filled, picked 2026-11-20, was too heavy on the phone): a 4 px bar in the up colour at limit up or the down colour at limit down at the screen's left edge (in the page margin, the row keeps its width), and the last price in that colour (`--zl-up` / `--zl-down`, as every change line); the rest of the row and the split-flap cards as on any row. The limit prices are the exchange's (MIS `u` / `w`, Fugle `priceHighLimit` / `priceLowLimit`), never worked out here; US rows have none. Holdings and Watchlist. Screens: "Holdings · limit rows", "Watchlist · limit rows". **Holdings rows sit on the summary's three columns** (Vellum's 3-column overlay over the Holdings screen, owner 2026-11-20): ticker and shares start on column 1, last price and today's % end on column 2's right edge, value and return end on the right margin.
- Money: one minus sign (U+2212) and one price format everywhere; a new ticker's currency comes from its market.

## 📡 Live data
- **Badge** beside every Invest title: **TW TRADING** / **US TRADING** names the market that trades and blinks its dot once per update; while none trades it names the next open of your tickers' markets, **US OPENS 22:30** (today) or **TW OPENS MON 09:00** (a later day), in device time; a weekday in the usual hours with the market shut reads **TW CLOSED · TYPHOON** / **US CLOSED · HOLIDAY** (also NO TRADING, EARLY CLOSE): `calc/calendar.ts`. **Opening bell:** the first update after the open sweeps the board once.
- **Live tick** (every 5 s at most while a market trades): Invest home, Holdings and Watchlist redraw under the no-blink rules; chart and holding pages change in place; other Invest pages show no live price. Shared code: `liveTick()` in `ui/c.ts`, `FlipDot.set`, `view.ts` `pricesMoved`.
- **Sources:** Taiwan stocks and TAIEX from TWSE MIS / Fugle (live), the TAIEX TODAY chart from the 5-second file; SOX, NASDAQ and US stocks from Yahoo. Any value that is late carries a delay tag; LIVE only when all is live (Source fix, open).
